ERP · Risk Management: Release digest

Covers: Financial Reporting Compliance 24C, Risk Management (Classic) 26D, Risk Management (Redwood) 26D
Oracle updated: 1 Sep 2026 · Source: Oracle Cloud Readiness

Executive Summary

The 24C and 26D releases for Risk Management bring a host of enhancements across Financial Reporting Compliance and both Classic and Redwood Risk Management modules. Notable updates include the introduction of advanced reporting dimensions for tracking controls, enhanced job listings, and the ability to manage access and internal controls more effectively. New features such as the Risk Cloud Daily Digest and improved access certification management streamline processes and enhance user experience. Customers should be aware of required actions related to the setup of new features and the transition to updated APIs.

What's New

  • Financial Reporting Compliance introduces enhanced question responses for survey questions, allowing for more detailed feedback in assessments. (Enhanced Question Responses)
  • New advanced controls job history dimensions have been added to OTBI, enabling better tracking of access and transaction job history. (New Advanced Controls Job History Dimensions)
  • Risk Management (Classic) now includes pre-built reports for Access Global Conditions and Access Entitlements, facilitating easier review of access conditions. (Access Global Conditions and Access Entitlements reports)
  • The Risk Management (Redwood) module features a new secured record access framework with roles for Risk Administrators and Users, enhancing control over record visibility. (Risk Management secured record access framework)
  • A new Risk Cloud Daily Digest feature consolidates relevant Risk Management activities into a daily email for users, improving task management. (Receive the Risk Cloud Daily Digest)
  • Participant Groups can now be created in Risk Management (Redwood) for easier management of stakeholder assignments across records. (Participant Groups and record access management)
  • New features for managing internal control records and certification campaigns have been introduced, allowing for better organization and tracking of internal controls. (Internal Control record management; Internal Control Certification campaign management)

What's Changed

  • Business object attributes and names in Risk Management (Classic) have changed to align with application data, requiring users to review existing controls that utilize affected business objects. (Business object changes)
  • The User Assignment Groups REST API will be replaced in a future release, prompting users to prepare for the transition by opening a service request for guidance. (IMPORTANT Actions and Considerations)

Deprecations, Retirements and Replacements

None identified in this release.

Action Required

  • Run the Report Synchronization job to utilize the new advanced controls job history dimensions in Financial Reporting Compliance. (New Advanced Controls Job History Dimensions)
  • Review existing controls that use affected business objects in Risk Management (Classic) and retire unsupported controls as necessary. (Business object changes)
  • Open a service request for details and guidance on transitioning to the new User Assignment Groups REST API. (IMPORTANT Actions and Considerations)
  • Assign the new roles (Risk Administrator, Risk Power User, Risk User) to manage access in the updated secured record access framework. (Risk Management secured record access framework)
  • Enable the preference for Advanced Access Request and assign required privileges to administrators and users for managing access requests. (Submit and manage Advanced Access Request)
