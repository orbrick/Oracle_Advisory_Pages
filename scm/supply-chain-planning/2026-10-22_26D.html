SCM · Supply Chain Planning: Release digest

Covers: Demand Management 26D, Sales and Operations Planning 26D, Supply Chain Collaboration 26D, Supply Planning 26D
Oracle updated: 25 Sep 2026 · Source: Oracle Cloud Readiness

Executive Summary

In the 26D release of Supply Chain Planning, major updates have been rolled out across Demand Management, Sales and Operations Planning, Supply Chain Collaboration, and Supply Planning modules. Key features include the introduction of intra-organizational material transfers, enhanced inventory management capabilities, and the ability to manage production campaigns. Customers are encouraged to perform pre-update tasks and can opt in to new features that may enhance their planning processes. Additionally, the release includes important actions regarding the migration to Redwood pages and updates to FBDI templates, which require attention to ensure a smooth transition.

What's New

  • Demand Management introduces the Business Growth Planning Workspace, enhancing the management of new product introductions. (Business Growth Planning Workspace)
  • Supply Chain Collaboration now supports intra-organizational material transfers for requests from Self Service Procurement, improving fulfillment flexibility. (Enable intra-organizational material transfers for transfer supply requests originating from Self Service Procurement)
  • Supply Planning adds the ability to manage production campaigns, allowing users to align manufacturing stages with campaign activities. (Manage production campaigns)
  • New features in Demand Management allow users to export plan status details to Excel, aiding collaboration and tracking. (Export details of the plan status)
  • The Supply Chain Network visualization is now available for supply plans, enhancing visibility into supply networks and planning results. (Analyze planning results using a Supply Chain Network visualization)
  • Users can now edit sourcing assignments in a spreadsheet, improving maintenance efficiency for high-volume updates. (Manage sourcing assignments in a spreadsheet)
  • New capabilities for managing minimum remaining shelf life constraints in Supply Planning ensure compliance with inventory quality standards. (Respect minimum remaining shelf life material constraints in Supply Planning)
  • The ability to filter planning visualizations by multiple product catalogs has been introduced, enhancing data analysis capabilities. (Filter by multiple product catalogs)

What's Changed

  • The toolbar in pivot tables has been enhanced to allow for quick access to filters and improved usability in comparison views. (Enable toolbar in pivot tables in compare mode)
  • Users can now work with plans during demand simulation, which was previously locked, improving user experience. (Work with plans during demand simulation)

Deprecations, Retirements and Replacements

  • The Routings page has been renamed to Operations as part of the transition to Redwood pages. (IMPORTANT Actions and Considerations)

Action Required

  • Customers must perform pre-update and post-update tasks as specified in Oracle documentation to ensure a smooth transition to the new release. (Pre-Update and Post-Update Tasks; Pre-Update and Post-Update Tasks; Pre-Update and Post-Update Tasks)
  • Users should opt in to new features delivered in this release to take full advantage of enhancements, using the New Features work area. (Optional Uptake of New Features (Opt In); Optional Uptake of New Features (Opt In); Optional Uptake of New Features (Opt In))
